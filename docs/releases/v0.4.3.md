## 这版做了什么

- 「检索后端不可用」这张卡不再骗人：引擎比 DSH 起得晚时，卡会在引擎答上来后自己消失；超时就说超时，不再说成「不可达」，也不再劝你去改一个对它无效的超时参数。
- 关掉的卡刷新后不会再回来（同一个标签页内）；手机或新标签页看到的是引擎此刻的状态，而不是程序启动那一刻的快照。

## 怎么做到的

- 启动检查等宿主把插件安顿好再探，只对「等一会儿可能就好」的失败重试；页面按时间等结果，取不到就再试，不搞「轮询几次就放弃」。
- 卡片显示前先问一次当前状态，显示后每 20 秒再问一次：两个引擎都好了就撤卡，只恢复一个就把那一行更新掉；这次问不到也继续排下一次，卡不会永久留在屏上。
- 超时与不可达在文案与诊断里分开；`EVERYTHING_TIMEOUT` 不再被写成「没在运行」。关掉的卡按标签页记住。

## What changed

- The "search backends unavailable" card stops lying: when an engine starts after DSH, the card removes itself once the engine answers. A timeout now says timeout instead of "unreachable", and no longer suggests a timeout knob that does not apply.
- A dismissed card stays dismissed across reloads (within the same tab); a second tab or the phone sees the engines' current state, not a snapshot taken at startup.

## How

- The startup check waits for the host to settle the plugin and retries only failures that can pass with time; the page waits by wall clock and keeps retrying instead of giving up after a fixed number of polls.
- The card is verified against the live status before it appears and every 20 seconds afterwards: both engines healthy removes it, a single recovery updates its row, and a failed check keeps polling rather than stranding the card.
- Timeout and unreachable are different words in both the card and the diagnostics; `EVERYTHING_TIMEOUT` is no longer reported as "not running". Dismissal is remembered per tab.

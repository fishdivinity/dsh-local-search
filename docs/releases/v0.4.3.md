## 这版做了什么

- 「检索后端不可用」的提示卡不再给出过时的结论：引擎比 DSH 起得晚时，提示卡会在引擎恢复后自己消失；超时就说超时，不再说成「不可达」，也不再建议改一个对探测无效的超时参数。
- 关闭过的提示卡刷新后不会再出现（同一个标签页内）；手机或新标签页看到的是引擎当前的状态，而不是程序启动时的状态。

## 怎么做到的

- 启动检查等宿主把插件加载完再探测，只对「等一会儿可能就好」的失败重试；页面按真实时间等结果，取不到就再试，不搞「轮询几次就放弃」。
- 显示提示卡前先查一次当前状态，显示后每 20 秒再查一次：两个引擎都好了就移除卡片，只恢复一个就把那一行更新掉；这次没查到也继续重试，提示卡不会一直留在屏幕上。
- 超时与不可达在文案与诊断里分开；`EVERYTHING_TIMEOUT` 不再被写成「没在运行」。关闭过的提示卡按标签页记住。

## What changed

- The "search backends unavailable" card no longer gives an out-of-date answer: when an engine starts after DSH, the card removes itself once the engine answers. A timeout says timeout instead of "unreachable", and the card no longer suggests a timeout setting that does not apply.
- A closed card does not come back after a reload (within the same tab); a second tab or the phone sees the engines' current state, not the state at startup.

## How

- The startup check waits for the host to finish loading the plugin and retries only failures that can pass with time; the page waits by real time and keeps retrying instead of giving up after a fixed number of polls.
- The card checks the live status before it appears and every 20 seconds afterwards: both engines healthy removes it, a single recovery updates its row, and a failed check keeps retrying instead of leaving the card on screen.
- Timeout and unreachable are different words in both the card and the diagnostics; `EVERYTHING_TIMEOUT` is no longer reported as "not running". A closed card is remembered per tab.
